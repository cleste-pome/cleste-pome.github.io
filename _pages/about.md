I am **cleste**, a researcher at **Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)**, Wuhan, China.

My research interest includes **multi-view clustering**, **representation learning**, and **computer vision**. I am particularly interested in robust multi-view learning under real-world data conditions, such as cross-view sparsity variations and manifold consistency.
